# Original-CNF polarity-aware pair-cutset admission (isolated research)

## Research problem and source evidence

The earlier original-QuPRS-Weighted-CNF pair-cutset experiment showed two distinct blockers on Feynman HWB6: a dense width-11 graph screen did not admit a pair under the bounded search, and repeated full min-fill planning imposed considerable cost. Reusing a source primal-graph bitset addresses the second cost, but the source pair-deletion graph remains an **overapproximation**: every clause containing a satisfied signed condition disappears from a true Boolean cofactor. Merely removing the two candidate vertices leaves those clauses' other variables connected even when that clause has already been satisfied.

An unsuccessful width-11 check on this overapproximation is **not** proof that every assignment-specific cofactor is also too wide. This is an experimentally falsifiable source-CNF graph-screen hypothesis, not a claim about the exact HWB6 cutset or a production improvement.

## New opt-in algorithm

Use the original source CNF and original signed complex literal weights without modifying the external CNF bytes, QuPRS encoder, original GPMC or production defaults.

- Require *both* \`--factor-pair-polarity-graph --factor-pair-fastgraph --factor-pair-cutset-ve\`, on the same research-only solver mode. The original dense-graph, shared-bitset overapproximation and polarity-aware arms remain separately callable for same-CNF native A/B.
- Preserve exactly the previous source constraints: original surviving scope <=320, original source clauses <=3000, root min-fill width 12..14, only the 12 highest original-primal-degree candidates and at most 24 pairs, 180 million ns total bounded planning. This includes original graph construction, all failed probes and every assignment-specific clause cofactor.
- For each pair (x,y), first attempt the previous conservative pair-deletion graph. If its min-fill bound <=11, retain the previous admission semantics without extra work. If it fails and the polarity arm is enabled, compute *all four exact Boolean clause cofactors* by deleting satisfied clauses and removing false tested literals; check their original surviving-variable primal graphs with shared bitset min-fill, including isolated/free variables. Only when all four are <=11 may the pair proceed to the original exact Dense Factor-VE arithmetic.
- Each of the four arithmetic branches contains all source weights for the other variables. Its conditioned pair weights \`w_x(a) * w_y(b)\` are owned exactly once at the outer Shannon sum. Boolean SAT is the OR of branches, separately from MPFR signed complex sum; nonzero, zero-cancelled, and UNSAT remain distinct.
- A failed admission, arithmetic cap or resource budget discards **all** partial branch work and invokes unchanged original Trail from the same source. Negative/censored results remain in full WallTime and output evidence. No default router, treewidth cap, source encoder or numeric tolerance changes.

## Independent exact structural + signed WMC witness

The new self-contained original CNF witness has 14 named variables: conditioned x=1, y=2 plus 12 others. For each of the four assignments to x/y, make exactly one of four distinct 11-cliques active, by 55 gated width-4 original CNF clauses. Thus there are exactly 220 original clauses. Across assignments their union is a 12-clique; the conservative deletion graph has bucket 12 and **rejects** a width-11 condition for (x,y). On each true assignment-specific Boolean cofactor, exactly one 11-clique remains, with the twelfth variable free, and all four branch graphs have bucket <=11. This cleanly distinguishes the two admission strategies; simply testing a 13-vertex original clique would not do so.

Run at 100 and 333 MPFR bits with dyadic positive/negative signed complex literal weights for x and y. Require **exact** SAT, real and imaginary equality with independently enumerated 2^14 assignments and original Trail. Also test exhaustive planner \`plan_all()\` parity against original dense min-fill on all 1024 five-vertex graphs, under several width caps. The original 13-variable two-cutset witness remains a regression.

## Native acceptance and go/no-go

Compare the new polarity arm with GPMC original unmodified C++ executable, Rust Inline, Dense VE, ADD, two Boolean BDD orders, single-cutset, old pair-cutset, and shared-bitset pair-cutset. All source CNFs are generated by the **pinned original QuPRS** encoder and then frozen before solver timing; the original signed complex weights, SAT requirements, original source hash, and identical precision contract (GPMC 30/100 decimal digits vs Rust MPFR 100/333 bits) remain unchanged. Seven order-rotated fresh child processes per regular Feynman source×precision on the same CI runner; 30 seconds / 2 GiB address-space each. Preserve full WallTime p50/p90, peak RSS, pair/cofactor graph probes, all real/imag counts and censor records (including Grover5).

A **GO for the research hypothesis** requires a demonstrably newly admitted pair on an unchanged hard original CNF, four completed branches, matched signed complex WMC and useful full WallTime/RSS against the same-run GPMC, followed by independent original MQT holdout. If only synthetic cases gain admission or original HWB6 still falls back, it is a structurally correct but hard-case **NO-GO**. Do not relax time/memory budgets or enable the route in production based on the synthetic witness.
