## Feature implementation and independent evidence

Only research work in `benchmarks/rust_core/src/quprs_printed_cnf.rs`; no `crates/rawmocs-core`, QuPRS, SolverAdapter, GPMC default, or CodSpeed identity changes.

- `parse_printed_dimacs(raw, Dialect::{Gpmc,Ganak})` accepts one `p cnf n m` header and one `c t wmc` marker, independent positive/negative literal `c p weight` declarations, then exactly `m` clauses. GPMC weights are `real imag 0`; Ganak weights are `real +/- magnitudei 0`. Missing literal weights each independently default to 1+0i; free/Tseitin variables retain their Full WMC factors.
- `printed_decimal` computes exact rational from a finite printed decimal, including scientific notation (`1.25e-2` is exactly 1/80) without f64. Bounds: 12 variables, 10,000 clauses, 400 kB source, 120-byte decimal, exponent absolute ≤120. Duplicate/out-of-range weights, missing marker/terminators, malformed comments, embedded zero, exponent overflow, nonfinite and unsupported format all error.
- `independent_exact_full_wmc` exhausts at most 2^12 assignments using explicit four-real-product rational arithmetic; does not invoke DPLL or `Gaussian::{add,mul}`. Compare the unmodified bounded DPLL solver with this independent SAT+count oracle at cache OFF and 10,000. Boolean SAT and exact complex WMC zero remain **separate** verdicts.

Golden expected source-defined cases × both dialects:
- Unconstrained full count **3/4**.
- Forced SAT **19/16 - 3i/16**.
- Contradictory Boolean UNSAT **zero WMC**.
- Disjunction **-5/16 + 9i/16**.
- Two-clause XOR-like relation **1/2 + 5i/16**.
- **SAT with exact complex cancellation**: full count zero, not Boolean UNSAT.
Additional controls: 0.6+0.8i printed decimal, source-test weighted disjunction 5/4, two free unweighted variables giving 4, empty clause proving UNSAT, a Ganak scientific-notation negative imaginary weight, and numerous malicious/malformed input refusal tests.

The exact decimal rational is an exact **printed token**, not a proof enclosing the source's transcendental quantum-phase coefficient. The QuPRS Rust exporter in [`rust/quprs/examples/interval_cnf_reference.rs`](https://github.com/PhysicsQoo/QuPRS/blob/0b28736651b3740ed3b201edd2efdef3da541e0d/rust/quprs/examples/interval_cnf_reference.rs) is a separate candidate for a future **actual encoder-emitted** source-capture experiment; this gate does **not** exercise it.
